About

I am a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind and a final-year PhD candidate at UIUC, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Jiawei Han. My research focuses on long-horizon agents and frontier evaluation. Recently, I have been most excited about agentic self-improvement.

During my PhD, I was a Research Fellow at Anthropic and interned at Google DeepMind, the Llama 3 post-training team at Meta, Microsoft Azure AI, and Microsoft Research. I also received the Amazon PhD Fellowship.
